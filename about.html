About The Lodge

A collaborative space for environmental leadership

Our Story

Surrounded by forests, rivers, volcanoes, and fjords, the University of Washington is home to exceptional programs in environmental science, law, and policy. Yet, despite the interconnected nature of environmental challenges and the likelihood that graduates of these programs will work alongside one another throughout their careers, opportunities for students to interact across disciplines remain surprisingly limited.

The Lodge was founded to bridge those divides. Through expert-led field trips, on-campus discussions, and social gatherings, we bring together students from across UW to explore the pressing environmental issues of our time, learn from one another, and build lasting connections across disciplines.

At the heart of The Lodge is a belief that responsible and effective environmental stewardship requires not only an understanding of science, law, and policy, but also a meaningful connection to the lands, waters, and communities our work affects. By getting outside together and learning directly from the people who study, manage, protect, and depend on these landscapes, we hope to foster a more collaborative, thoughtful, and effective generation of environmental professionals.

Above all, The Lodge is about building community, exploring the incredible landscapes of the Pacific Northwest, and having fun along the way. Whatever your academic background or career aspirations, if you care about the environment, there is a place for you here.

Our Mission

🌍 Interdisciplinary Learning

Break down barriers between science, law, and policy by creating opportunities for students to learn from one another, develop literacy outside their own disciplines, and approach environmental challenges from new perspectives.

🏕️ Experiential Education

Take learning beyond the classroom and into the forests, rivers, coastlines, and communities of the Pacific Northwest, exploring firsthand the complex environmental issues that shape our region.

🤝 Community & Engagement

Build relationships across campus and beyond by connecting students with researchers, land managers, policymakers, tribal nations, community organizations, and other environmental practitioners.

💡 Environmental Leadership

Cultivate a generation of environmental professionals who value collaboration, understand the landscapes and communities their work affects, and are equipped to tackle complex challenges together.

Our Field Trips

Each quarter, The Lodge explores a different environmental theme — what we like to call an "ology." Through expert-led outings and complementary on-campus events, we examine environmental issues from scientific, legal, policy, and community perspectives. Our field trips might take us anywhere from the old-growth forests of western Washington to the agricultural landscapes of the Columbia River Basin or the tidepools of the Pacific coast.

🌲 Forests & Forestry

Explore forest ecology, timber management, wildfire, biodiversity conservation, and the legal and policy frameworks shaping the future of Washington's forests.

💧 Water & Agriculture

Learn about water allocation, agricultural practices, water quality, and the challenges of balancing human and ecological water needs across the region.

🏞️ Lands & Conservation

Explore protected landscapes, habitat restoration, land-use decisions, and innovative approaches to biodiversity conservation.

🐟 Rivers & Fisheries

Examine salmon recovery, watershed health, river restoration, and the intersection of fisheries science, resource management, and environmental law.

🌊 Oceans & Coastlines

Explore marine ecosystems, coastal communities, shellfish and fisheries management, and the challenges of protecting our marine environments.

🌾 Communities & Stewardship

Learn from tribal nations, local communities, and other practitioners about the relationships between people, natural resources, governance, and environmental stewardship.

Who We Are

The Lodge is a student-led community spanning disciplines, departments, and degree programs at the University of Washington. Our members bring diverse academic backgrounds, professional interests, and personal experiences, united by a shared curiosity about the natural world and a desire to make a positive environmental impact.

Our community includes students from:

  • School of Law
  • School of Aquatic and Fishery Sciences (SAFS)
  • School of Environmental and Forest Sciences (SEFS)
  • School of Marine and Environmental Affairs (SMEA)
  • Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering (CEE)
  • Evans School of Public Policy & Governance
  • Other programs across the University of Washington

You don't need to study environmental science, law, or policy to join us. Undergraduate and graduate students from all disciplines are welcome, and no prior experience is necessary. Bring your curiosity, your perspective, and your friends!
